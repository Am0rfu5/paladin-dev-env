## WARN-01 — Herald reach

Herald's service-level wiring exists on Formation and Phalanx (19 references each) and is absent
from Campaign, Chain of Command, and the Commander router (0 references each). The milestone's
own composite flow — *"a developer runs a Chain of Command battalion end-to-end and sees commander
delegation with a normalized result and Herald-formatted output"* — therefore does not compose
through the shipped `Commander` API without the caller invoking a Herald directly.

**This does not falsify GAP-03.** The trait method is pattern-agnostic —
`format_battalion_result(&self, result: &BattalionResult)` at `herald.rs:85`, implemented by all
three Heralds — and `BattalionResult` is the shared type every pattern produces. Any pattern's
result *can* be rendered; only the ergonomic `with_herald` wiring is missing on three services.
GAP-01 and GAP-04 (commander delegation, normalized result) are independently wired at
`commander.rs:480,798-831`.

Recorded as tech debt with a suggested owner rather than a blocker.
