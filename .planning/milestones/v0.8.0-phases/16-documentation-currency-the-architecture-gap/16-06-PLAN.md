<objective>
Close DOCS-02: `docs/src/appendix/design-and-architecture.md` gets a **recorded disposition** — it
is archive material, and it says so and says where to look instead — and the substance of the gap is
closed by giving Sentinel a home in the live architecture chapter.

Purpose: the file has been invisible for two milestones. It is still exactly 311 lines with zero
mentions of Commander, Council, Conclave, Grove, Maneuver, Sanctum or Sentinel and zero Mermaid
blocks, because Milestone 11 Epic 2 relocated it into the appendix and Epic 3's non-goals then
exempted the appendix from rewriting. The relocation put the corpus's largest documentation gap into
the one chapter nobody was required to fix. It cannot stay both archive and deliverable.
Output: ADR-0047, an archive banner, Sentinel coverage in the live chapter, and PROMOTION.md advanced.

**Why the substance is one component, not seven (M-03, measured):** `docs/src/architecture/` (5
pages, 1,216 lines) already covers Commander, Sanctum, Maneuver, Council, Conclave and Grove, and
`docs/src/appendix/sentinel.md` covers Sentinel at 976 lines. Against the 19 shipped
ubiquitous-language components the live chapter covers **18 of 19**; only Sentinel is absent from it.
Rebuilding the 311-line appendix would create a second architecture document competing with the live
chapter — the exact duplication that produced this gap (D-01 rationale).

**Scope guards:** D-00a — ADRs live in `.planning/decisions/`, flat sequential numbering, seven
headings, **no frontmatter**. D-00d — amend in place, dated, superseded text retained. D-00g —
contested positions get ADRs. D-03 — **do not author diagrams into a file being archived**. D-00o —
commit with `--no-verify`. The five live architecture pages are **not** audited for currency here
(CONTEXT.md Deferred Ideas).
</objective>
