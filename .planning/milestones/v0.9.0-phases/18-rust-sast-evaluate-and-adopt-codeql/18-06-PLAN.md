Correct a live documentation defect in the ruleset procedure, then decide the promotion on the
numbers already recorded and carry it through every place the required-check set is written down —
in one change, or record the hold with its trigger condition.

A required-check set that disagrees with itself across the committed ruleset, the live ruleset, the
document's table and the document's prose is exactly the defect SAST-03 was written to forbid. So is

one that is already applied.

clear the threshold written before the measurement, the scanner stays advisory and the criteria
become a named open item with its trigger condition — the alternative, silence, is what the
milestone audit criticised.

Purpose: SAST-03's promotion clause, end to end.
Output: a corrected re-application procedure, and either a 45-context required set consistent
across four places, or a recorded hold that says exactly what would change the answer.
</objective>
