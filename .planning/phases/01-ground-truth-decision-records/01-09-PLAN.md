<assumption_delta_decision>
The detector fired `kind: chosen` on the phase goal's phrase "naming the chosen variant". That signal
is a false positive for the ADR work — recording which already-shipped variant is authoritative
changes no identity model.

There is, however, a genuine derived→chosen transition inside this gap closure, and it is recorded
here rather than left implicit:

- **Noun now primary:** the *recorded coverage scope*.
- **Decision:** `promote`. The scope was previously **derived** — whatever
  `.github/workflows/integration-tests.yml` happened to run. Because that CI scope cannot execute in
  this environment (no Docker), the scope becomes an explicitly **chosen**, written-down parameter of
  ADR-0006: one workspace-wide line-coverage number under named exclusions. The chosen scope is
  promoted to primary and the CI invocation is demoted to one instance that must conform to it — which
