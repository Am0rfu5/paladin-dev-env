<objective>
Run the five bench targets this workspace actually ships, derive the percentile families criterion
does not compute, measure the two metric families criterion cannot produce, and record all of it as a
new dated section in the baseline document that already exists.

Purpose: QUAL-05 and ROADMAP criterion 5 require `cargo bench` to complete and a baseline document to
record throughput, P50/P95/P99 latency, memory-per-Paladin and startup time — "so the next
performance change can be compared against something." Criterion reports mean, median, MAD and
confidence intervals; it reports neither P95, P99, memory nor startup. Rather than leave three of the
four metric families blank or fabricate them, this plan derives the percentiles from criterion's own
on-disk per-iteration samples and measures memory and startup from a purpose-built harness, stating
the source of every family (D-12/D-13/D-14).

Output: `examples/muster_baseline.rs`, and a new dated run section in
`docs/src/appendix/performance-baseline.md` with the 2026-05-27 run retained as explicitly superseded.
</objective>

## Recorded decisions this plan carries

- **The measurement harness is sited at `examples/muster_baseline.rs`, not in `benches/` and not in
  `tests/`.** `examples/` is auto-discovered by cargo, so this adds **zero** Cargo target
  declarations; ADR-0006's `--ignore-filename-regex` already excludes `(^|/)(examples|benches)/`, so
  the harness cannot enter the coverage denominator or move plan 03-07's exit figure; and a new
  `[[bench]]` entry could shift the `--object` discovery count that Research Pitfall 3 warns must
  stay stable. `muster` is the medieval-military term for assembling troops, which is what the
  harness does to Paladins.
- **The bench invocation keeps the 2026-05-27 run's flags** (`APP_ENV=test`, `-- --noplot`) plus
  `--offline`, so the *methodology* is comparable even though the *figures* are not.
