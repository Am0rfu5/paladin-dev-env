<objective>
Close CLOSE-02's Epic 24 cluster `1.0` — the missing ChainOfCommand benchmark — and record its
baseline.

Purpose: `battalion_benchmarks.rs` registers Formation, Phalanx and Campaign benchmarks but no
ChainOfCommand benchmark at all, while `docs/src/appendix/battalion-benchmarks.md` claims at three
separate lines that "ChainOfCommand Benchmarks: Compiling and enabled" and names three specific
cases. That is a doc-vs-tree contradiction, not a documented deferral. D-12 resolves it by making
the doc true rather than retracting it; D-13 requires the new target to be measured and recorded so
the baseline document does not silently cover 5 of 6 battalion patterns.

Output: a `benchmark_chain_of_command` function registered in the existing `criterion_group!`, and a
new dated run section in `docs/src/appendix/performance-baseline.md`.

**Reading of D-12, stated so it is not mistaken for scope creep.** D-12 says the point of writing
the benchmark is to make the existing doc claim *true*. That claim names three cases —
`2_levels_3_subordinates`, `2_levels_5_subordinates`, `wide_10_subordinates`. A single benchmark
case would leave two thirds of the sentence still false, so `benchmark_chain_of_command` registers
three `c.bench_function` calls, one per named case. This is one function, as D-12 specifies, and it
is an addition to satisfy the decision fully, not a reduction of it.

**Dependency.** This plan depends on 06-02, which adds a Herald field to
`ChainOfCommandExecutionService`. Measuring before that lands would record a figure that does not
describe the shipped tree.

**Executor note:** `.planning/config.json` sets `workflow.worktree_skip_hooks: true`. Do not
disable it — without it every commit cold-compiles the whole workspace inside the worktree.

**Spec-less probe fallback.** This plan carries none of the eight edge-probe rows (they land in
06-01, 06-03, 06-05 and 06-07). One prohibition survived Stage-2 precision and is authored
descriptor-less above.
