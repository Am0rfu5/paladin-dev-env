<objective>
Close 06-VERIFICATION truth 10 for the three published records: reconcile ADR-0013, `CHANGELOG.md`
and `.planning/PROJECT.md` with the behaviour that plan 06-08 actually made reachable from
`GroveExecutionService::execute()`.

`RoutingStrategy::LlmRouting` without setting `routing_model` gets `BattalionError::RoutingError`.

only path a real operator uses — the error was intercepted and a fallback agent was substituted
instead. The documents described the intended design rather than the shipped behaviour. Plan 06-08
makes the claim true; this plan makes the records precise about *where* it is true, cite the
exercisers that prove it, and record how the gap was found and closed.

the shipped mechanism and the scope boundary.

`## Considered Options` and `## Checkpoint outcome` sections are read and preserved, not rewritten.
