<objective>
Land the measurement spine of Phase 15: a dedicated `coverage` job in `ci.yml` that measures the
workspace under D-01's scope behind Redis and MinIO, mirrored byte-identically by a new `make
coverage` target — plus the two remaining PIPE-01 gates (`cli-tests`, `bench-check`) and the
Makefile targets PIPE-03 names.

Per **D-04**, this plan lands the coverage job in **measure-only mode** (no `--fail-under-lines`).
That is not a reduced version of the gate: Docker is absent from every authoring environment
(verified 2026-08-13), so the figure the floor must be derived from can only be produced by CI
itself. Plan `15-03` captures that figure and turns the gate on. The absence of the threshold flag
is a value that gets filled without any structural change to the job.

Per **D-11** this is one phase, wave-decomposed, with PIPE-01 … PIPE-05 landing in waves 1-4 and
DEFER-01 … DEFER-03 in waves 5-7. That order is not a planner choice: the ROADMAP fixes it, and the
register's own reasoning is that Epic 25 "establishes quality gates that validate all subsequent
work". This plan is wave 1 — the gates come before the coverage work they measure.

Purpose: the project stops asserting its quality and starts measuring it on every push.
Output: three new CI jobs, one new Makefile section, five new/extended Makefile targets.

**Executor note:** `.planning/config.json` sets `workflow.worktree_skip_hooks: true`. Honor it —
otherwise every commit in this phase cold-compiles the whole workspace through the pre-commit
clippy hook.
</objective>

<assumption_delta_decision>
**Signals surfaced (advisory, non-blocking):** `pluralization: second` ("the coverage register comes
second"), `pluralization: another` ("rather than writing another one-off"), `chosen` ("the coverage
threshold has one number with one rationale, chosen between…").

**Primary noun:** *the coverage floor* — the single binding, policy-bearing threshold number.

**Decision: `no-change`.**

Rationale: the two pluralization signals are ordinary English prose — "second" orders two epics and
"another" describes one-off mocks; neither names an emerging generalized identity. The `chosen`
signal is the only real candidate, and it points at a noun that was **already promoted**: ADR-0006
made *the coverage floor* a first-class concept with a single canonical home, an explicit derivation
rule (truncate toward zero), an explicit comparison (at-or-above) and a ratchet clause, back in
Phase 1. This phase re-measures its **value** under a wider feature scope and amends that one record
in place per D-00l. No new identity appears, so nothing is promoted. Manufacturing a promotion here
would mint a second coverage record and recreate precisely the multi-number failure RECON-07 exists
to close.
</assumption_delta_decision>
