## Accomplishments

- **D-03 (trigger surface):** `push.branches` in `ci.yml`, `feature-flags.yml` and `pre-commit.yml` rewritten from a four-prefix allowlist (`main`/`develop`/`feature/**`/`release/**`, or `main`/`develop`/`release/**` for pre-commit.yml) to the single match-all pattern `'**'`. `docs.yml` (`push: [main]`) and `release.yml` (`push.tags: ['v*.*.*']`, no `branches` key) — the two deliberate D-03 exceptions — are byte-for-byte untouched. Every `on:` key present before the edit (`push`, `pull_request`, `workflow_dispatch` where applicable) is still present after it.
- **D-10 (examples job cache-miss fix):** `ci.yml`'s `examples` job (`name: Example Muster (Feature Matrix)`) had its three consecutive `actions/cache@v4` steps (`Cache cargo registry`, `Cache cargo index`, `Cache cargo build`) replaced by one `Swatinem/rust-cache@v2` step (`cache-on-failure: true`), followed by a new `Fetch dependencies` step (`cargo fetch --locked`) placed before the first of the four build steps asserting `--offline`. All four `--offline` flags are preserved untouched — `grep -c -- "--offline" .github/workflows/ci.yml` returns exactly `4`.
- **D-11 (RUSTSEC-2026-0249 governance):** `deny.toml`'s `[advisories].ignore` array gained one entry (`RUSTSEC-2026-0249`) in the unmaintained group, with an inline comment naming the crate and its transitive path. `SECURITY-EXCEPTIONS.md` gained a matching `[[exception]]` row, adjacent to the existing rust-s3-family rows (`RUSTSEC-2026-0194`/`-0195`), carrying all eleven mandatory governance fields (`id`, `class`, `crate`, `path`, `why_present`, `why_not_fixable`, `owner`, `review_date`, `scope`, `compensating_control`, `revisit_condition`). `cargo deny check advisories` now exits `0` (previously exited `1` with `error[unmaintained]: smartstring is unmaintained`), and `./scripts/check-advisory-register.sh` exits `0`.
- The operational edge this creates was recorded, not engineered around, per the plan's explicit instruction: because `License & Dependency Policy` becomes a required check under D-05, a newly-published RustSec advisory can now turn it red with no repository change at all. Demoting the unmaintained class to a warning was rejected.
- Cross-checked and left alone, per plan scope: `cargo deny check advisories` also emits a non-blocking `spin 0.9.8` yanked-crate warning — a different, non-gated class, out of this phase's scope.
