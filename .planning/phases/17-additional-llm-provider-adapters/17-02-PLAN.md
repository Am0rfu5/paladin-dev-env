<objective>
Record PROV-01 as a decision rather than a preference. The verdicts themselves are already locked
(D-01, D-02, D-03) — this plan turns them into a durable, citable artifact with the scoring criteria
written down *before* the scores, every candidate carrying exactly one verdict with its reason, and
the four user-named candidates (Kimi, Gemini, Qwen, Meta/Llama) each explicitly dispositioned
including the Llama row's host question.

**Purpose:** PROV-01's "done when" clause is a documentation deliverable, and ROADMAP criterion 1
is verified by a human reading it. Without this artifact the phase ships five adapters whose
selection rationale lives only in a discussion transcript, and the next request for Groq or Bedrock
becomes a new phase instead of a pointer at D-03's already-covered verdict.

**Output:** `ADR-0045` (the study), an amended `PROV-01` row in `REQUIREMENTS.md`, and an advanced
`PROMOTION.md` allocation.

**Why an ADR and not only ledger rows (D-00g, Claude's Discretion in 17-CONTEXT.md):** the contested
part of this study is **D-03's rejection of Groq / Together / Mistral / Fireworks / Bedrock as
*already covered* rather than deferred** — that is a position a future reader will want to argue
with, and it is exactly what D-00g routes to an ADR. The uncontested verdicts (Kimi, Qwen, Grok,
Ollama, Gemini all build) are recorded in the same table because splitting the field across two
artifacts would make the study unreadable. **D-11's breaking default-build change is the phase's
*second* contested position and gets its own ADR-0046 in plan 17-06** — one ADR per contested
position, so a future phase revisiting the flag decision does not have to supersede the provider
study (the coarse-supersession-unit cost ADR-0034 already paid once).

  human-selected in an interactive `/gsd-discuss-phase 17` session on 2026-08-16. Say so.
